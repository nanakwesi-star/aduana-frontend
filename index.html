Headmaster's Message

A school built on discipline, care, and real results.

"At Aduana Model JHS, we believe every child deserves a school that takes their future as seriously as their family does. Our teachers know each learner by name, our results are tracked and verified at every stage, and our doors are always open to parents who want to be part of that journey."

We combine strong academic fundamentals in Mathematics, English, Science, and Computing with the values of respect, punctuality, and honesty — the same standards we hold our own results process to.

Start Your Application
1Visit the school office or contact us to collect an admission form.
2Submit the completed form with your child's birth certificate and previous school report.
3Attend a brief placement interview with the Headmaster.
4Receive confirmation and your child's official admission number.
